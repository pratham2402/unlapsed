Data control

Your documents stay yours.

The PDF is read in memory and deleted after extraction. Only structured fields are stored.

What is stored

  • Document type, issuer, holder name, last four digits, issue date, expiry date, and constraints.
  • Source excerpts and page numbers for each extracted fact, so every value can be checked.
  • Official requirement sources with the date they were checked.
  • The plan, tasks, and activity log.

The uploaded PDF itself is not written to disk in the demo. Deletion removes the extracted record and its facts.
